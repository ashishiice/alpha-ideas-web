✗ Downside Risks

  • Budget 2027 (1 February): another excise or cess hike is the same shock that cost the stock 20% in January 2026. This is the one event that can gap through the stop.
  • Volume erosion: legal industry volumes fell 7.5% YoY and ITC has flagged a sharp rise in smuggled cigarette seizures. If illicit trade takes permanent share, the franchise damage outlasts the price hikes.
  • Dividend risk: FY27 EPS is ~₹15 against a ₹14.50 payout — a ~95% payout ratio. A cut would remove the 5.1% support and force a re-rating lower.
  • Input costs: sugar, copra, palm oil and wood stay elevated; FMCG margin expansion stalls.
  • Value-trap risk: ITC is 47% below its 2024 peak and staples are structurally de-rated. A reversal in FII flows would hit the highest-yielding large-cap first.
  • Gap risk: a tax headline can open 10-15% below any stop; the stop limits the normal case, not the tail.
