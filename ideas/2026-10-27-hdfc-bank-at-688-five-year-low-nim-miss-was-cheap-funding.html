🔍 Why This Works Now

1
The 8bp NIM "miss" was a liquidity-timing artifact, not competitive erosion. Q2 FY27 net interest margin fell 8bp QoQ to 3.16% on total assets (3.32% on interest-earning assets) explicitly because of FCNR(B) flows. Indian banks mobilised $132.98 billion of FCNR(B) deposits before the RBI's 31 August 2026 deadline, and the RBI's scheme let them swap those dollars at a fixed 3.5% per annum for a three-year minimum tenor — 100bp below the prevailing market swap rate. HDFC Bank's share of that arrived as rupee liquidity in Q2 that had not yet been deployed against 16.4% loan growth. You pay the carry in the quarter you raise it and earn the spread for the following three years. The market booked the cost and ignored the asset.
2
Core earnings grew 12.6% while headline PAT grew 4.7%. The entire gap is treasury: trading gains collapsed to ₹780 crore from ₹2,390 crore year-on-year as the 10-year went to a 3.5-year high. That is a mark-to-market consequence of the rate cycle, not a credit or franchise problem. Asset quality actually improved — GNPA 1.11% (from 1.17% QoQ and 1.24% YoY), NNPA 0.37%, credit cost 0.44%, provisions ₹3,570 crore.
3
Rate hikes are a NIM tailwind for this specific book, not a headwind. Roughly 70% of HDFC Bank's loans are EBLR/floating. Repo went 5.25% to 5.50% on 7 October and the market prices another 100bp by February 2027. A floating book reprices within one reset cycle — typically one quarter — while deposit costs reprice with a two-to-three quarter lag. That mechanical gap is the source of the margin recovery, and it means this position pays you even if yesterday's "RBI won't deliver 100bp" bond call is wrong.
4
The leadership overhang expires today. Anup Bagchi, 56 — 34 years at the ICICI Group, former Executive Director of ICICI Bank and until now MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life, IIT-Kanpur and IIM-Bangalore — takes charge as Managing Director and CEO for a three-year term from 27 October 2026. Jagdishan's term ended 26 October. Five years of dead money was substantially about governance and succession after the chairman's abrupt exit; that specific discount has nothing left to price.
5
Valuation has decoupled from book-value compounding. Book value is ₹391 per share and has compounded every year; the stock is ~8% below where it traded in October 2021. That is a P/B de-rating from roughly 3.5x to 1.76x against an essentially unchanged 13.6% ROE — the multiple moved, the business did not.
