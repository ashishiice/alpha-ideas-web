Since February the RBI has been trapped: a weak rupee (94.20/USD) drives imported inflation, so defending the currency means hiking, but hiking into a war-driven oil shock kills growth. The market has already begun to validate this: USDINR peaked at 97.05 and is back at 94.20, a 2.9% rupee recovery, while the RBI's reserves and the equity market both held. Oil falling lets the RBI stop hiking *and* keep the rupee. That is the trade.
