⚖️ Risk-Reward Analysis

✓ Upside Factors

  • Coupon rises on every NSC rate increase — an RBI hiking cycle is a tailwind, not a mark-to-market risk
  • 8.05% base coupon is 100-250 bps above comparable sovereign-grade alternatives (G-Sec 7.05%, bank FDs 6.00-6.75%)
  • Asymmetric reset math: G-Sec yields up ~50 bps in the last quarter alone; a 25-100 bps NSC increase at the Dec 31 review takes the coupon to 8.3-8.95%
  • Sovereign guarantee — the only risk-free accrual at this yield available to retail
  • Semi-annual cash payout (Jan 1 / Jul 1) — no reinvestment gap at the reset dates

✗ Downside Risks

  • Liquidity lock-in: no premature redemption for regular investors; senior citizens (60+) can exit only after year 6 with a 50% interest penalty
  • Coupon could drop if the rate cycle turns: if RBI pauses and G-Sec yields fall back, the NSC rate can be cut at future quarterly reviews, lowering the coupon at each Jan 1/Jul 1 reset
  • Full slab taxation with TDS: interest is taxable at slab and TDS applies once annual interest crosses ₹10,000 — on ₹5 lakh, ~₹40,500/yr, post-tax ~5.8% for a 30% bracket
  • No 80C deduction — unlike NSC itself, the FRSB does not qualify for Section 80C
  • Opportunity cost: if G-Sec yields spike then collapse (pause-and-cut), a fixed 10-year bought at 7.05%+ could outperform on duration gains the FRSB structurally cannot capture

Risk Mitigation

  • Allocation cap — 30% of the fixed-income sleeve (max ~₹3 lakh of a ₹10 lakh bucket): the non-tradability is the main risk; never let an instrument you cannot sell exceed one-third of the defensive book
  • Barbell for liquidity: pair the FRSB with a liquid/overnight fund or RBI Retail Direct T-Bill roll so short-term cash needs never force decisions about the locked portion
  • Tax-aware sizing: in the 30% bracket, weight toward this idea only for money that would otherwise sit in taxable FDs at 6-6.75% — the ~150 bps gross spread survives tax; prefer PPF/SSY headroom first for tax-free accrual
  • Form 15G/15H: if your income is below the taxable threshold, file to stop TDS on the January 1 / July 1 payouts
  • Decision discipline, not a stop-loss: there is no price to stop out of — the only "exit trigger" is the December 31 NSC review; if NSC is held at 7.7% or cut, direct fresh increments to fixed G-Secs at 7%+ instead, and let the existing position accrue
