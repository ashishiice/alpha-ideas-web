The H1 miss is being fixed in real time. CIL produced 321 MT in April-September, down 2.5% YoY, which is exactly why the stock de-rated to 7.5x. 2
The FY27 target needs ~85 MT/month for five months — hard, but no longer implausible. To reach 815 MT, CIL needs about 427 MT in November-March. The usual November-March peak plus a normal monsoon recovery is the difference between a miss and a meet; the market is pricing a miss.

E-auction volumes rose 50.6% YoY to 22.8 MT, the high-margin part of the mix.
