The crash is an accounting event, not an asset-quality event. But the actual results beat: standalone PAT ₹5,170 crore (+8% YoY, +7% QoQ), NII ₹11,000 crore (+20% YoY), NIM up 3bps QoQ to 2.55%. RBI’s final ECL Directions (issued April 27, 2026, effective April 1, 2027; PRID-backed Master Directions) permit the Day-1 transition adjustment to be added back to CET1 and phased in over four financial years (25% per year, FY28-FY31). Canara management itself guided recognition over ~2 years, subject to yield-curve conditions. RoA may dip below 1% transiently, but capital is not destroyed — CET1 buffers across PSU banks run 2-8% above minimums (CareEdge: capital impact ≤30bps).

Every comparable regulatory-overhang event for PSU banks (NPACL clean-up 2018-2020, COVID reserve build) resolved with re-rating to 0.7-0.9x book once the accounting fear peaked and actual credit costs stayed contained.
