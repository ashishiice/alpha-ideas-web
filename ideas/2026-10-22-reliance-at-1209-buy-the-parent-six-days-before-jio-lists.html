✗ Downside Risks

  • O2C squeeze. Brent at $109 raises working capital and feedstock cost, and fuel-export uncertainty has been an overhang on RIL for most of this year.
  • Retail slowdown. Discretionary weakness has been cited as a full-year drag; a poor festive quarter would compress the sum-of-the-parts.
  • Rate headwind. With the 10-year at 7.44% and the RBI tightening, multiple expansion for a leveraged conglomerate is hard work.
  • Mechanical supply. Funds buying Jio in the listing week may fund it by trimming RIL — price-insensitive selling exactly when the trade needs support.
  • Single-name, single-date risk. This is one company and one binary event, not a diversified position.
