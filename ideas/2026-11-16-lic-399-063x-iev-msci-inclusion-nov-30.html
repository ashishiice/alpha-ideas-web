✗ Downside Risks

  • Growth is genuinely weak: individual APE fell 4.1% in Q2 and 65.98% of individual APE is still par business, which structurally caps margin expansion.
  • If the RBI goes past 6.00% — a 50bp move on 4 December, or a hawkish February — the bond book takes another mark-to-market hit and IEV falls for a third consecutive year.
  • Post-inclusion fade: index flows are one-off. MSCI additions routinely give back 5-10% in the fortnight after the effective date.
  • Government supply: GoI at 90% must reach 25% by 2032, and an early OFS announcement would cap the stock regardless of fundamentals.
  • Equity risk: LIC is the market's largest domestic equity holder, so a Nifty correction hits both IEV and the dividend cover at the same time.
  • Regulatory: IRDAI's new commission regime could reset unit economics across the industry if the pass-through is worse than assumed.
